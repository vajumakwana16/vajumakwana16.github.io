Privacy Policy

📅 Last updated: August 10, 2025 • Puzzle Master

This Privacy Policy explains how Puzzle Master ("we", "us", or "the App") collects, uses and shares information when you use our mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account or submit personally identifiable information to use the core game. However, some information may be collected automatically by the app or third-party services we use:

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and other system information.
  • Usage data: game sessions, level completion, crashes, and other anonymous analytics data used to improve the app.
  • IP address and approximate location: may be collected by third-party advertising networks to serve ads and for fraud prevention.

2. Ads and Third-Party Services

We use third-party services (such as Google AdMob) to serve advertisements. These services may collect information about your device and usage to provide personalized or non-personalized ads in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Ad providers may use cookies, device identifiers, and similar technologies. To learn more about Google's data practices, see Google's Privacy Policy.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and maintain the game
  • Understand and analyze usage patterns and crash reports
  • Display ads and measure ad performance
  • Improve features and user experience

4. Data Retention

We store local game data (e.g., levels completed, stars) on the device using local storage (SharedPreferences). We do not store personal data on our servers. Third-party services may retain data as described in their privacy policies.

5. Children

The app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will promptly delete it.

6. Your Choices

You can:

  • Opt-out of personalized ads through your device ad settings or by visiting Google Ad Settings.
  • Uninstall the app to stop app-based data collection.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after material changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
