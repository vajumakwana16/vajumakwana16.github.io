🎮 Privacy Policy - Color Quest Kids

Last Updated: January 11, 2026

Quick Summary: Color Quest Kids is designed with your child's privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from our users.

📱 Introduction

Welcome to Color Quest Kids! This privacy policy explains how we handle information in our educational game app designed for children ages 3-8. We are committed to protecting your child's privacy and complying with all applicable privacy laws, including COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act).

🔒 Information We Do NOT Collect

Color Quest Kids does NOT collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal information (names, addresses, email addresses, phone numbers)
  • Photos or videos
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers for tracking purposes
  • Contact lists
  • Microphone or camera access
  • Any other personally identifiable information

💾 Local Data Storage

The app stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Game Progress: Levels completed, stars earned, and scores
  • Settings: Sound and music preferences
  • Achievements: In-game accomplishments

This data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third parties. If you uninstall the app, all local data is deleted.

📢 Advertising

Color Quest Kids displays advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain information for ad serving purposes:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • General location (country/region level only)
  • Ad interaction data

Important: We use AdMob's child-directed settings to ensure ads are appropriate for children and comply with COPPA regulations. AdMob does not use personalized advertising for child-directed apps.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit: Google Privacy Policy

🛍️ In-App Purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases to:

  • Remove advertisements
  • Unlock premium features

All purchases are processed through Google Play Store. We do not collect or store any payment information. Please refer to Google Play's privacy policy for information about how payment data is handled.

🌐 Internet Permissions

The app requires internet permission only for:

  • Displaying advertisements (if ads are not removed)
  • Processing in-app purchases

The app functions fully offline except for these features.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parental Controls

Parents can:

  • Disable sound and music in the app settings
  • Remove ads through in-app purchase
  • Monitor game progress locally on the device
  • Uninstall the app at any time to delete all local data

🔐 Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit personal information, there is no personal data at risk. All game progress is stored locally on your device using secure Android storage mechanisms.

🔄 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

👶 COPPA Compliance

Color Quest Kids is designed for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is configured to use child-directed settings for all third-party services.

🌍 International Users

This app is available worldwide. Since we do not collect personal information, GDPR and other international privacy regulations regarding data collection do not apply. Local game data remains on your device regardless of your location.
