Arrow Escape Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arrow Escape ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us"), developed by Vaju Makwana, handles and protects your information when you use our mobile application on Android and other supported platforms.

By downloading, installing, or using Arrow Escape, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Collection and Storage

Personal Data: Arrow Escape does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise physical location. You can play all levels of the game offline without registering or creating an account.

Local Device Storage: The Application stores your game progress, earned stars, user settings (Dark Mode, Sound FX, Background Music), and hint/undo balances locally on your device using standard Android SharedPreferences. This data remains on your device and is never uploaded to remote servers.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

We use Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to serve banner, native, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements that help support the development of this free game. Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous data such as:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID / GAID), IP address, device model, and OS version.
  • Ad Interaction Data: Impressions, clicks, and video completion metrics.
  • Diagnostics: Performance metrics and crash logs necessary for delivering reliable advertisements.

For more details on how Google collects and processes data, please visit Google Privacy & Terms and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Advertising Controls and Opt-Out

You can manage and personalize your advertising preferences directly from your device:

  • On Android: Open Settings → Google → Ads and choose Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.

Opting out stops personalized ads; you may still receive general, contextual ads.

4. Children’s Privacy

Arrow Escape is a casual logic puzzle game suitable for all audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Our third-party advertising SDKs are configured in compliance with Google Play's Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You retain full control over your locally stored game data:

  • You can clear all progress and stars at any time inside the app via Settings → Reset Progress.
  • Uninstalling the game removes all local data permanently from your device.
