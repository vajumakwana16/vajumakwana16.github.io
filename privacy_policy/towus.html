1. Introduction

Welcome to Towus ("we", "our", or "us"). Towus is a private, secure messaging application designed to let you communicate in real time with the people you trust. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect information in connection with your use of the Towus mobile application (the "App").

By installing or using Towus, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of the App.

📋

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that is necessary to provide and improve our services. This falls into two categories: information you provide directly and information collected automatically.

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google Sign-In.
  • Profile Data: Any display name, bio, or avatar photo you choose to add within the App.
  • Messages & Media: Text messages, images, videos, audio recordings, files, and documents you send or receive through the App.
  • Location Data: If you choose to share your location in a conversation, we temporarily process your geographic coordinates to generate a shareable location message. Location is only shared when you explicitly initiate it.
  • QR Code Data: When you scan a QR code to add a contact, the code content is processed locally on your device.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (for push notifications), and mobile network information.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens are collected solely to deliver message notifications to your device.
  • Usage Data: Online/offline presence status, message delivery timestamps (sent, delivered, read), and typing indicators — used only to power real-time messaging features.
  • App Diagnostics: Crash reports and performance data to help us identify and fix bugs (collected via Firebase Crashlytics if enabled).

4. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We use Google Firebase (Firestore, Storage, Auth, Cloud Messaging) to operate the App. These services process data on our behalf under strict data processing agreements.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by law, court order, or governmental authority, we may disclose information to comply with legal obligations.
  • Safety: To prevent fraud, abuse, or imminent harm to users or others.
  • Business Transfer: In the unlikely event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your data may be transferred. We will provide notice before this occurs.
Your messages are yours. We do not read, analyze, or use the content of your private conversations for any purpose other than delivering them.

5. Data Security & Encryption

Protecting your data is a core priority. We implement the following safeguards:

  • Cryptographic Encryption: Sensitive data within the App is encrypted using industry-standard cryptographic algorithms via the cryptography package.
  • Secure Storage: Authentication tokens and sensitive credentials are stored in the device's secure enclave using flutter_secure_storage.
  • Biometric Authentication: Towus supports biometric lock (fingerprint / face recognition) to prevent unauthorized access to the App on your device.
  • Screen Protection: Screenshots and screen recording are restricted within the App to prevent unauthorized capture of private conversations.
  • Firebase Security Rules: All Firestore and Storage data is protected by strict server-side security rules — only authenticated users can access their own data.
  • HTTPS / TLS: All data in transit between your device and our servers is encrypted with TLS.

While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

🗂️

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service:

  • Messages & Media: Stored on Firebase servers until you or the recipient deletes them, or your account is deleted.
  • Account Data: Retained until you delete your account through the App.
  • Push Tokens: Updated or removed when you log out or uninstall the App.
  • Location Data: Only processed momentarily to create a location message; we do not store a history of your locations.
  • Diagnostic Logs: Retained for up to 90 days for bug-fixing purposes.

Upon account deletion, your profile information and associated data are permanently removed from our active systems. Some residual data may remain in backups for a limited period before being overwritten.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Withdraw consent for location sharing or notifications at any time through your device settings.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address in the Contact Us section. We will respond within 30 days.
