Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2025

FocusX ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how the FocusX mobile application handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

We do not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as name, email address, phone number, or location.

b. App Data (Stored Locally)

FocusX stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Tasks, reminders, and habits
  • Completion status, streaks, and productivity data
  • User preferences such as theme and notification settings
  • Expense or productivity-related records (if enabled)
This data remains on your device and is not shared unless you explicitly enable backup or cloud sync features.

c. Notifications

If enabled, notification permission is used only to deliver task and habit reminders. We do not read or collect notifications from other applications.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly to:

  • Provide core app functionality
  • Send reminders and alerts
  • Generate productivity analytics and streak insights
  • Improve app stability and performance

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

3. Data Storage & Security

All app data is stored securely on your device. Industry-standard security practices are used to prevent unauthorized access.

If cloud services (such as Firebase) are introduced, data will be encrypted during transmission.

4. Third-Party Services

FocusX may use trusted third-party services for analytics, crash reporting, or AI-based features. These services receive only minimal, anonymized data when required and operate under their own privacy policies.

5. Permissions

  • Notifications: Used for reminders and alerts
  • Storage (optional): Used for backups or exporting data

All permissions are optional and can be managed from your device settings.

6. Children’s Privacy

FocusX does not target children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete all app data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app
  • Disable permissions and notifications at any time

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
